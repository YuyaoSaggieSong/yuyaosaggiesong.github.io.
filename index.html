About

I am a biology student focused on working at the intersection of biological science and artificial intelligence. My primary interest lies in solving the risk of turning manipulated tissue into cancerous tissue when attempting to return cells to their initial state. By extracting regeneration mechanisms from transverse fission, I aim to apply safer protocols to human bone tissue regeneration.
